# HF Notes

Visual thesis: an Apple-inspired personal space with full-width frost-gray sections, centered oversized typography, a single blue interactive accent, and one photographic laptop showcase.

Content plan: journal identity and paired reading/about actions; two filterable opening notes; a short factual introduction and one confirmed milestone; calm, centered article pages.

Interaction thesis: staggered headline entrance, subtle scroll-linked product-image depth, and restrained hover/selection feedback. Respect reduced-motion settings.

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/aecac5da-f397-4ddf-b71f-de1efc434cb8

- Background #f5f5f7; text #1d1d1f; action fill #0071e3; links #0066cc.
- Use local system sans-serif fonts. No proprietary font redistribution or Apple branding.
- Centered full-width sections, generous spacing, thin rules, rounded action buttons.
- Body at least 17px; article measure approximately 680px.
- No analytics, external fonts, private contact data, or unverified biography.
- The product image is a locally served Unsplash photograph, not the author's device or workspace.
